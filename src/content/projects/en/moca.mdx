moca is a fictional streetwear brand inspired by Istanbul’s coffee culture. The products in the store aren’t photos or 2D mockups: every garment was sewn from flat pattern pieces, draped on a hanger with a cloth simulation and rendered in real time 3D in the browser.

I wanted to bring the feeling of standing in front of a clothing rack to the screen. Garments hang sideways; hover one and it turns to face you while its neighbours gently swing.

## Shopping from the rack

- There are two racks: T-shirts, and Long Sleeves & Sweatshirts, with 8 products each.
- Hovering a garment turns it around its hanger hook. Neighbouring hangers slide along the rail and swing like pendulums.
- Clicking opens a detail view: the background blurs like frosted glass, the product can be dragged to rotate 360°, and arrows or the keyboard move to the next item.
- There’s also size selection, a cart, a free-shipping bar and a demo checkout. On mobile the rail scrolls with your finger.

## How the garments were made

All geometry is generated offline with Node scripts before it reaches the browser. The browser only downloads the finished models (GLB).

1. **Pattern:** Front and back bodies and sleeves are drawn as 2D polygons with real measurements. Collar, cuff and hem allowances are marked by edge type.
2. **Triangulation:** Each piece becomes an even mesh with roughly 1.3 cm spacing using constrained Delaunay triangulation.
3. **Simulation:** An XPBD-based cloth solver handles stretch and bending constraints, seams, contact with the hanger and self-collision.
4. **Post-processing:** Seams are smoothed, real fabric thickness is added and ambient occlusion is baked with ray tracing.
5. **Export:** With compressed attributes, each garment ends up at about 280–360 KB.

## Garments never touch

When a garment turns forward, its sleeves mustn’t pass through its neighbours. Each model is sliced into 7 cm horizontal bands to build a “footprint”. Every frame, those footprints are rotated with the garment’s current turn and swing, and the closest distance two neighbours can sit without touching is computed; hangers slide to exactly those positions.

I stress-tested it with fast, slow and random mouse movement. The smallest measured gap is 1.3 cm, with no overlap in any frame.

## How the fabric looks

The fabric is drawn with layers added on top of Three.js’s `MeshPhysicalMaterial`: jersey knit texture, storage creases, rib and twin-needle stitching, raised screen prints, heather yarn and cotton sheen.

All textures and print designs are generated in code at runtime; no image files are downloaded. The fonts used in the prints are subset to include Turkish characters.
